1. Local-First Desktop Software Architecture

HENU OS Records Management desktop software operates on a 100% local-first paradigm. When you install and run HENU OS on your computer:

  • All cooperative society profiles, member names, addresses, distinctive share numbers, and financial records are stored entirely on your local hard drive (within %APPDATA%\HENU_OS_Records\).
  • No member data, master workbooks, or generated statutory PDFs are ever transmitted to, processed on, or stored on external cloud servers or third-party databases.
  • The software operates completely offline without requiring internet connectivity for core operations.

2. Website Information Collection

When visiting this official product website (henuos.com or local instance), we do not collect personal identifying information unless you voluntarily submit it through:

  • Feedback Submissions: Information provided (name, email, feedback description) is used strictly to diagnose software bugs and improve application features.
  • Contact Inquiries: Contact details are used exclusively to respond to your technical support or deployment inquiries.

3. HENU AI Assistant & Chatbot Interactions

The HENU AI Assistant operates client-side using an indexed static knowledge base derived from official documentation.

Chat interactions are processed in real-time in your browser session. Conversation history is cleared when you close or reload the browser window.

4. Cookies and Web Storage

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We utilize standard browser localStorage solely for functional preferences such as theme selection (Light / Dark mode).

5. Data Security Standards

We implement rigorous technical safeguards including modern HTTPS encryption, strict Content Security Policies, and input validation to protect all website visitors.

6. Privacy Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please reach out through our Contact Us page.
